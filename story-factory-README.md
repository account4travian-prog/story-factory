# Story Factory: an idea-to-concept pipeline with gates that can fail

A 7-stage pipeline that turns a raw story idea into a developed, chapter-ready concept, built because "the AI filled in the gaps" is how story projects quietly rot. Upstream of the [chapter pipeline](https://github.com/account4travian-prog/chapter-pipeline).

**My role:** development authority, every creative ruling is mine. Sol (GPT) works the in-progress development; Atlas runs interview design, capture, verification, and the mechanical gates, and never invents creative content.

## The rules that make it an evaluation system
- Each stage **passes on evidence or sends the work back**, triage can shelve a project, the completeness check can fail it, and acceptance is a decision I make on the record.
- **OPEN slots stay OPEN.** Anything undecided is recorded as undecided; no model may silently fill it in. A finished-looking document with invented content is a failed document.
- Proposed and accepted are different states, and the record keeps them separate.

The design rests on research I directed and reviewed: 22 story frameworks, writers'-room practice, 9 published AI pipelines, and 8 papers, plus a failure catalog from those pipelines, and three gaps the plan states honestly (no taste gate, no re-planning mechanism, no quality metric yet).

## Proof run, the Sokcho trilogy
Three proposals built on one real event: in July 2016, Sokcho, South Korea was the only place in the country where Pokémon Go worked, and the town transformed overnight.
- **The Summer of the Glitch**, grounded novella (the full run below)
- **The Ontological Bleed**, horror serial (intake stage)
- **The Silicon Safari**, cyberpunk serial (intake stage)

Summer of the Glitch ran Stages 1–6 (2026-10-29): premise locked at revision 3 through **15 formal rulings**, story document approved, cast and form locked, a dry chapter-1 intake **passed with no improvisation**, the exit test that the handoff bundle works as a task specification.

## The research dossier as a grading artifact
The run is backed by a 236-claim research dossier (127 confirmed at build), then attacked by a falsification pass with independent verifiers: **33 claims survived as stated, 9 were weakened, 1 was refuted**, and one beat that could not be verified was kept in the story only by my explicit ruling, tagged as unverified in the record. That distinction, *verified, weakened, refuted, ruled-on*, is the factory working as designed.
