# Story Factory: an idea-to-concept pipeline with gates that can fail

A 7-stage pipeline that turns a raw story idea into a developed, chapter-ready concept, upstream of the [chapter pipeline](https://github.com/account4travian-prog/chapter-pipeline).

**My role:** development authority, every creative ruling is mine. Sol (GPT) takes creative lead once a project enters the chapter pipeline; Atlas runs interview design, capture, verification, and the mechanical gates, and never invents creative content.

## The gates that can fail
- Each stage **passes on evidence or sends the work back**: triage can shelve a project, the completeness check can fail it, and acceptance is my decision on the record.
- **OPEN slots stay OPEN.** A slot I have not decided is recorded as undecided, and a restricted critic pass catches any slot a model quietly filled with a plausible guess.
- Every file carries a PROPOSED label until I accept it.

The exit test is one sentence: a dry chapter-1 intake runs on the handoff bundle with no improvisation. The downstream pipeline is the judge, not the factory's own report.

The design rests on research I directed and reviewed: 22 published story frameworks, writers'-room practice, 9 GitHub AI story pipelines, and 8 papers, plus a failure catalog from them. The plan states three gaps it could not close: no validated taste gate, no validated re-planning mechanism, no validated quality metric.

## Proof run, the Sokcho trilogy
Three proposals built on one real event. In July 2016 Pokémon Go was not available in South Korea, but it worked in Sokcho, a coastal city north of the 38th parallel, because the city's cell in Niantic's regional grid sat in the northern service region. For about two weeks Sokcho was the country's only door into the game; buses sold out and hotels surged.
- **The Summer of the Glitch**, grounded novella (the full run below)
- **The Ontological Bleed**, horror serial (proposal and intake file)
- **The Silicon Safari**, cyberpunk serial (proposal and intake file)

Summer of the Glitch ran Stages 1 to 6 (2026-10-29): premise locked at revision 3 through **15 formal rulings**, story document approved, cast and form locked, and a dry chapter-1 intake **passed with no improvisation**, so the handoff bundle works as a task specification on this run.

## The research dossier as a grading artifact
The run is backed by a 236-claim research dossier (127 confirmed at build, 85 single-source, 24 unverified or folklore). A falsification pass over the mechanism track's **46-claim register** returned **33 survived as stated, 9 weakened, 1 refuted, 3 cannot-verify**. One beat that could not be verified was kept in the story only by my explicit ruling, tagged as unverified in the record.
